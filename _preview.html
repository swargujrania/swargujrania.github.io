Remote Usability Testing

The audience mobile experience was tested through remote usability testing sessions to gather feedback and
evaluate the design idea.

What worked

Improvements

• Some thought the tech-powered in-concert
session was too disruptive for a classical music
concert that feels “analog” to some.• Some aspects can be more streamlined e.g. pre-filling/saving phone number.• Possible survey fatigue.

• A higher response rate can be expectedcompared to traditional feedback process.• The incentives to answer surveys with the

ASO Credits system.

• The design was very easy and
straightforward, it requires very low effort.• In-the-moment pre-concert survey helps fillthe idle waiting gap before the concert• Using a phone to answer the survey is moreconvenient than filling out a paper survey.• No app download required, no accountcreation required.

• The post-concert survey might get the least

response: people will be in a hurry to leave the
hall and go back.• The actual value of the ASO credits should beclear for users to decide whether to take the timeto fill the survey.• Buying more ASO Credits should be an option.
